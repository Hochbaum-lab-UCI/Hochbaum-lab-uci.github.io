We study how microbes live, respond, and build, and we use what we learn to create new materials and technologies.

Our work spans four connected threads:

- **Microbial nanowires.** How bacteria such as *Geobacter sulfurreducens* assemble cytochrome nanowires to move electrons outside the cell.
- **Bioinspired materials.** Designing self-assembling and other materials inspired by microbial appendages.
- **Microbial stress responses.** How bacteria sense and respond to antibiotics, osmotic stress, and other stressors.
- **Sensing technologies.** Reading out changes in bacterial metabolism to detect antibiotic susceptibility and environmental contaminants.
